## Welcome!

This is the first post powered entirely by Markdown files stored in the repository. Whenever new content is pushed to GitHub, the blog updates automatically during the next deployment.

### Why Markdown?

- **Version Control** – Every edit lives alongside the code.
- **Review Friendly** – Pull requests show the full history of changes.
- **Portable** – Markdown renders beautifully across different platforms.
